HydroTerra Field Data Compiler v0.30 - HYPACK LOG import

Step 2 now accepts .RAW, .LOG, and .ZIP files.

Implemented behavior:
- Multiple HYPACK LOG files may be loaded.
- LOG files are parsed as survey organization sources, not sensor observations.
- RAW references are extracted in their listed order.
- Referenced RAW files are compared to the loaded/scanned RAW inventory.
- Missing referenced RAW files create warnings.
- Loaded RAW files not listed in a LOG are reported informationally.
- The RAW inventory is ordered by the first available LOG ordering where possible.
- LOG reconciliation appears on the Import page and in the Word report.
- Original LOG files are included under 01_Original_Data/HYPACK_LOG in compiled packages.
- RAW files remain the source of truth for positions, depths, devices, quality, offsets, and line geometry.
