Version 0.22 rule-explanation validation

- Survey Lines now includes a Warnings column with concise reasons.
- Selecting a line displays a Rule Inspector for coverage, offline tolerance, positioning, depth, and configuration.
- Missing QUA data is reported as NOT EVALUATED / Information rather than automatically warning the line.
- Non-fixed observations do not automatically warn a line when the configured fixed-percentage threshold is still satisfied.
- Yellow row highlighting now corresponds to the explicit warning summary shown in the row.
