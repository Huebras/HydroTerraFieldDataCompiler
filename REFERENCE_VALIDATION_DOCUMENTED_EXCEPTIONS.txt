v0.27.3 documented exception workflow
- Bar check and SVP requirements apply only when a single-beam survey type is selected.
- Magnetometer-only and other non-single-beam projects do not receive these requirements.
- Missing single-beam bar-check or SVP files require a written reason on Step 9.
- A completed reason changes the package item to Documented exception (warning) and permits report/package export.
- Reasons are included in the Word report and package README.
