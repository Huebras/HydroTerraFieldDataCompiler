Navigation integrity gap correction validated against 080426 Dredge Area Hydros.zip.

Previous rule:
  gap > 1.5 x median POS interval

Problem:
  Median interval was approximately 0.10 seconds, so ordinary 0.20-0.80 second
  HYPACK record spacing was incorrectly counted as missing navigation. This caused
  29 of 30 RAW files to receive the maximum 30-point gap penalty and score 70%.

Corrected rule:
  gap > max(1.0 second, 5 x median POS interval)

Reference dataset:
  Maximum observed within-segment interval was approximately 0.80 seconds.
  These intervals are now treated as normal logging jitter rather than dropouts.
